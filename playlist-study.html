🧠 Why Lofi Helps You Study

Research shows that lofi music — with its consistent tempo, soft beats, and minimal lyrics — activates the brain's alpha waves. These waves are associated with relaxed alertness, the ideal state for absorbing information, solving problems, and staying in flow for extended periods. Unlike silence (which can feel tense) or lyric-heavy music (which distracts), lofi hits the sweet spot.

🎧 What Makes a Good Lofi Headphone?

For lofi music, you want: warm bass response (lofi relies on low-end warmth), a wide soundstage (for that "room" feeling), comfortable ear cups for long sessions, and ideally closed-back design to block distractions. All picks below score high on these criteria.
